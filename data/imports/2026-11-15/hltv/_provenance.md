# Provenance of these captures

`xlsx_<mapstatsid>.json` were generated by script from `hltv_full_statlines.xlsx` (8 HLTV map-stats pages, 80 player rows), which was **transcribed from screenshots** of the pages, not produced by `docs/hltv-capture/capture.js`.
Kills, headshots and deaths are copied as printed; the map score is bo3.gg's own score for the same map number (the transcription has no score), and map numbers are inferred from the mapstatsid order (confirmed against the maps bo3.gg lists).
Checks that stand in for a capture run: the printed `K (hs)` / `D (t)` text equals the split columns on all 80 rows; no player has more headshots than kills; on every map each team's total deaths equal the other team's total kills to within 0-1 (the importer's own kills-vs-deaths check);
the per-match over counts of the legs settled from them equal an independently settled run of the same matches (FaZe-fnatic 7/18, HEROIC-Magic 10/20, Eternal Fire-100 Thieves 10/19, PARIVISION-33 11/17).
