Kills, headshots and deaths are copied as printed; the score (13-3) is bo3.gg's for the same map. The page lists 11 players (tex1y 0 kills and heis999 4 kills share a NEM seat), so the importer's 10-player check is waived for this map only, by `_accept.json`.
Checks that stand in: all 11 players agree with bo3.gg's own table (heis999 appears there as "163") except bluewh1te, who is 14 kills / 9 HS on HLTV and 13 / 8 on bo3.gg; the user confirmed 16 headshots (9 + 7) for bluewh1te's Maps 1-2 HS leg on 2026-11-21.
